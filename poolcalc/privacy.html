Privacy policy

Pool Chemistry Calculator & Log · effective October 20, 2026

This page is the privacy policy for the Android app Pool Chemistry Calculator & Log, published by Jaison Arts, package com.jaisonarts.poolcalc.

Who we are

Pool Chemistry Calculator & Log is published by Jaison Arts for Android. The package name is com.jaisonarts.poolcalc.

Data that stays on your phone

Pool names, volumes, water tests, notes, unit choices, and reminder settings are stored only on the device, in a local database and app preferences. The app has no account, no cloud backup of its own, and no server run by Jaison Arts. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes that data.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on secondary screens, an occasional full-screen ad after you have used the calculator for a while (never on the first session), and an optional rewarded ad if you choose to unlock a step-by-step treatment plan. Google may process an advertising ID, IP address, and device information to deliver and measure ads. Pro removes ads. See Google’s policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles payment details. The app stores an entitlement on the device so Pro stays unlocked, and you can restore it from the Play account that bought it. Jaison Arts does not receive your card number.

Notifications and ratings

Test reminders are scheduled on the device. They are not sent through a push server. The Play in-app review prompt, if shown, is handled by Google.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
